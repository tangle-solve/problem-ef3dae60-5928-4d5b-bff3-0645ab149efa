### CLIM-000259: State-dependent sensitivity in ancient warm climates
- **Subfield:** Paleoclimate and long-timescale dynamics
- **Open:** Infer changing feedbacks across icehouse and greenhouse climates while separating slow ice, vegetation, geography, and weathering effects from fast-feedback sensitivity.
- **Status:** The 2024 PhanDA reconstruction assimilated proxies across 485 million years; feedbacks and forcings remain incompletely separable.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Tests climate physics outside the modern observational envelope.
- **Key works:** Judd et al. (2024), [“A 485-million-year history of Earth’s surface temperature”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk3705).
- **Related:** Bayesian paleodata assimilation; changing-boundary inverse problems.
